# Photo check

Check every face photo **before** analyzing it. Color and grooming advice built on a bad photo is wrong advice delivered confidently. A 10-second check, and one request for a better photo when needed, prevents that.

## What a usable photo needs

| Check | OK | Not OK → why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **Face visible** | Front-facing or slightly turned, eyes visible, whole face in frame | Profile, cut off, far away → can't judge face shape, beard lines or eyes |
| **Light** | Natural daylight (window, overcast outdoors), even | Warm indoor bulbs, neon, colored LEDs, direct sun with hard shadows, flash → skin and undertone colors are distorted |
| **No filters** | Unedited camera photo | Beauty filter, black-and-white, strong color grading, portrait-mode glow → undertone and skin texture are fake |
| **Sharp and large enough** | Face fills a good part of the frame, in focus | Blurry, very small, heavy compression → no detail for beard density or eye color |
| **Nothing covering** | Clear glasses are fine; hair and beard as usual | Sunglasses or tinted lenses, cap shading the face, mask, hand over the face |
| **Neutral surroundings** | Neutral background and top preferred | Strong colored wall or a top in a saturated color right at the chin → it reflects onto the skin (a mild cast only lowers confidence) |
| **Consent** | His own photo, or he agreed | Photo of someone who didn't agree → don't analyze |

## Verdict

- **Good:** all checks pass. Analyze, with confidence up to "high".
- **Usable with limits:** one minor issue (mixed light, colored top, slight angle). Analyze, say which finding is affected, cap that finding's confidence at "medium" or "estimate", and say once what would improve it.
- **Not usable:** any major issue (face not visible, filter, sunglasses, strong colored or artificial light, blurry, consent unclear). Don't analyze. Say in one or two sentences what's wrong, and ask for **one** new photo with the tips below.

If the second photo is still not usable, don't ask a third time. Do what the photo allows, mark everything as an estimate, and suggest checking the results in a mirror in daylight.

## Asking for a better photo

Keep it short, friendly and concrete (adapt to the user's language):

> The photo doesn't work for a color analysis yet: [reason, e.g. the warm lamp makes your skin look more yellow than it is]. Could you send one more?
> - Stand facing a window in daylight (no direct sun), no flash, no filter
> - Face and neck filling about half the frame, looking into the camera
> - Ideally a plain white or grey top
> - Clear glasses are fine; no sunglasses or cap

## What to record

In `STYLE-PROFILE.md`, note only the verdict and its effect, e.g. "Photo: mixed window/office light → color confidence medium". Never store the photo itself, links to it, or descriptions of his body beyond what fit needs.
